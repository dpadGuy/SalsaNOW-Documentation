Donations are welcome and optional.

They help keep development going, including time spent helping people with SalsaNOW and GeForce NOW, and the monthly GeForce NOW subscription used to maintain the project.

## Discord role

Send proof of a donation in DMs and you can get a special role on the SalsaNOW Discord.
