Change the desktop wallpaper from SalsaNOW Settings. You can use one of the built-in pictures, or a picture of your own.

## Built-in wallpapers

<Steps>

1. Open the Start menu and choose **SalsaNOW Settings**.

   <Shot
   	src="/images/start-menu-settings.png"
   	alt="Start menu with SalsaNOW Settings highlighted"
   	caption="SalsaNOW Settings in the Start menu"
   />

2. Open **Personalization**, then **Background**.

   <Shot
   	src="/images/wallpaper-settings-home.png"
   	alt="SalsaNOW Settings home with Personalization highlighted"
   	caption="Personalization is in the sidebar"
   />

   <Shot
   	src="/images/wallpaper-personalization.png"
   	alt="Personalization page with Background highlighted"
   	caption="Open Background"
   />

3. Select a picture under **Personalize your background**.

   <Shot
   	src="/images/wallpaper-backgrounds.png"
   	alt="Background page with a grid of built-in wallpapers"
   	caption="Built-in backgrounds"
   />

   <Shot
   	src="/images/wallpaper-builtin-desktop.jpg"
   	alt="Desktop using a blue built-in wallpaper"
   	caption="The selected picture becomes the desktop"
   />

</Steps>

The same page has **Bing wallpaper of the day**. Turn that on to use today's Bing photo. It stays off until you switch it on.

## Your own picture

<Steps>

1. On the Background page, choose **Browse backgrounds**.

   <Shot
   	src="/images/wallpaper-browse.png"
   	alt="Background page with Browse backgrounds highlighted"
   	caption="Browse backgrounds"
   />

2. Pick a `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, or `.bmp` file, then choose **Open**.

   <Shot
   	src="/images/wallpaper-file-picker.png"
   	alt="Choose a photo window open to the Downloads folder"
   	caption="Choose a photo from the session"
   />

3. That picture is now the desktop wallpaper.

   <Shot
   	src="/images/wallpaper-custom-desktop.jpg"
   	alt="Desktop using a custom cat photo as the wallpaper"
   	caption="A picture you chose"
   />

</Steps>
