Desktop shortcuts save with SalsaNOW. When you add, remove, or rename one, it syncs right away.

<Badge text="Needs persistent storage" variant="caution" />

<Aside type="tip" title="Already on">

Saving starts on its own. SalsaNOW keeps the shortcuts for you.

</Aside>

Deleted and renamed shortcuts are kept here:

```text
I:\Apps\SalsaNOW\Backup Shortcuts
```
