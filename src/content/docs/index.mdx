<Aside type="note" title="Independent project">

SalsaNOW is an independent project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to NVIDIA or GeForce NOW in any way.

</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Which accounts work">

SalsaNOW runs on accounts that include Install-to-Play. The free tier and alliance partners are left out. The [FAQ](/faq/#why-are-install-to-play-games-missing) lists those partners.

</Aside>

<Aside type="caution" title="Bans">

The chance of a ban is extremely small. No one has reported a ban for using SalsaNOW.

The risk is still there. NVIDIA does ban accounts for actions such as AFK farming and privilege-escalation exploits.

No one is responsible for any punishment NVIDIA gives you.

</Aside>
